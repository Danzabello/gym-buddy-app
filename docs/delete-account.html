Delete your Gym Buddy account

Last updated: November 11, 2026

You can delete your Gym Buddy account yourself in the app, or ask us to do it by email.

In the app

  1. Open Gym Buddy and sign in.
  2. Go to the Profile tab.
  3. Tap Account, then Delete Account.
  4. Read the list, type DELETE, and tap Delete My Account.

You are signed out at once and your account is removed.

Without the app

Email danielsgymbuddy@gmail.com from the email address of the account, with the subject "Delete my Gym Buddy account" and your username if you know it. If you signed in with Google, use your Google email address. We delete the account within 30 days and tell you when it is done.

What is deleted

  • Your profile and sign-in account, including the Google name and picture address stored with it if you signed in with Google
  • Your workouts and check-ins, streaks and break days, experience points, coins and cosmetics
  • Your buddy team memberships, friendships, nicknames, nudges and invites you sent
  • Your device token and notification settings

What stays

  • A team's streak record stays with the remaining team members, no longer linked to you.
  • A workout your buddy created with you stays on your buddy's account, with the link to you removed.
  • Records held by Google, such as crash reports, are governed by Google.

If you signed in with Google

Deleting your Gym Buddy account does not delete your Google account. You can review the apps you sign in to with Google in your Google Account settings.

Accounts that were never finished

If you create an account but do not finish setting it up, we delete that account and its email address automatically after 30 days. The 30 days count from the day you created it or last signed in, whichever is later.
