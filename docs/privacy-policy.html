Privacy Policy for Gym Buddy

Last updated: November 11, 2026

Gym Buddy ("the App", "we", "us") is developed by Daniels Zabello, Ireland. This policy explains what the App collects, why, who can see it, how long we keep it, and what you can do about it.

Gym Buddy is free to use. We do not sell your data and we do not show ads.

Questions or requests: danielsgymbuddy@gmail.com. To delete your account, see Delete your account.

1. Information We Collect

Account

You can create an account with an email address and a password, or with Google. If you use email and password, we store your email address; your password is handled by our sign-in provider, Supabase, and we cannot read it.

Sign in with Google

If you choose "Continue with Google", Google's sign-in screen on your phone asks you to pick a Google account. Google gives the App a sign-in token, which we pass to Supabase to create or find your Gym Buddy account. The token identifies your Google account and its email address. Supabase also stores the name and picture web address that Google includes in the token; the App never reads or uses them, and they are deleted with your account. You choose a display name, a username and an avatar in the App, like every other user. We do not ask Google for access to your contacts, calendar, files or any other Google data.

Profile

During setup you give us a display name, a username and your age (you must be 16 or over), and you choose an avatar from the App's preset list. The App also stores your phone's timezone, so that your daily streak and check-in times are correct. Your profile also holds a fitness level and a "looking for a buddy" setting; the App sets both to default values, and older accounts may also hold a workouts-per-week answer from an earlier version.

Earlier versions of the App also asked for fitness goals and gender (both optional). The App no longer asks; if you answered, those answers are kept until you delete your account.

Activity

We store the activity you log: workouts (type, date and time, planned and actual length, and an optional note of up to 500 characters), check-ins, streaks, break days, and the experience points, level, coins, cosmetics and achievements you earn. Please do not put health conditions or other sensitive details in notes. We do not collect weight, height, heart rate, calories, sleep or other health measurements.

Buddies

We store your friend requests and friendships, your buddy teams, the invite codes you create or use, nudges between buddies, and nicknames you give friends.

Notifications and device

To send push notifications we store a device token from Firebase Cloud Messaging and your notification settings. We keep a copy of each notification's text inside the App for 7 days so that you can read it again. The token is removed when you log out or delete your account.

Crash reports

In the released App, Firebase Crashlytics (Google) collects crash and error reports: the technical error and where it happened in the code, your phone model, Android version, App version, and an installation identifier created by Crashlytics. We do not add your name, email address or account to these reports, and we do not add any custom information to them. The error message itself is produced by the App and its software libraries, and in some cases it can include a technical identifier, such as an account identifier or a username that was part of a failed request. Google governs how long these reports are kept.

Invite links

When someone opens an invite link, we record the IP address of the request for a short time, to stop automated guessing of invite codes. These records are deleted automatically 10 to 11 minutes after the request, and they are used for nothing else.

On your phone

The App keeps on your phone: your sign-in session, your theme choices, a few short-lived markers it needs to work (for example, whether you have checked in today), and, if you arrived through an invite link, the invite code until you use it or finish setup. If you installed the App from Google Play through an invite link, Google Play passes the invite code to the App once; the App keeps it only if it matches the invite-code format.

What we do not collect

We do not access your location, camera, photos, microphone or contacts. The App has no advertising or analytics services, and we do not use an advertising identifier.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • Run your account and the App's features (sign-in, streaks, check-ins, buddies, teams), including replying when you contact us. Our legal basis is contract: we need this information to provide the App to you (Article 6(1)(b) GDPR).
  • Send push notifications. Our legal basis is your consent, which you give when you allow notifications. You can withdraw it at any time by turning notifications off in your phone's settings or in the App's notification settings (Article 6(1)(a) GDPR).
  • Prevent abuse and diagnose crashes (for example, invite-code guessing and App errors). Our legal basis is legitimate interests: keeping the App secure and working (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR).

Giving us an email address or Google account, and an age of 16 or over, is needed to use the App. We do not use your information for advertising, we do not sell or rent it, and we do not make automated decisions about you that have legal or similarly significant effects.

3. Services We Use

Supabase hosts our database, sign-in and server code. Our project runs in the United Kingdom (London, eu-west-2).

Firebase Cloud Messaging (Google) delivers push notifications. Google receives your device token, the notification title and text, a notification type, an identifier of the item the notification is about (for a nudge, this is the account identifier of the person who sent it), the sender's display name and avatar choice, and colour and style hints.

Firebase Crashlytics (Google) receives crash reports, as described above.

Google Sign-In (Google Play services on your phone) handles the sign-in screen when you choose Google.

Google Play distributes the App and tells it the invite code from an invite link when you install through one. Google's own policies apply.

GitHub Pages hosts this website and the invite page; GitHub receives the web address and IP address of visitors. These pages use no cookies, no analytics and no fonts from other websites.

We do not use any other third-party services.

4. Coach Max

Coach Max is an automated character, not a person. He appears in your buddy list, joins a team with you and checks in once a day. What he says is written in advance and chosen by our own systems; no message is generated by an outside AI service and none of your data is sent to one. The App marks him "AI" only so that you can tell he is not a real user. His notifications use the same push system as everyone else's.

5. Who Can See Your Information

Gym Buddy is a social fitness app, and some information is visible to other users by design:

  • Your profile: every signed-in user can see your username, display name, avatar and avatar border, fitness level, whether you are looking for a buddy, your workouts-per-week setting, and the date you joined. Other details on your profile, such as your age, email address, gender and fitness goals, are not visible to other users. Your timezone is shared with your team members so that shared check-in times are correct.
  • Check-ins are visible to you, your team members and your accepted friends.
  • Team streaks are visible to the members of that team only.
  • Workouts are visible to their owner and to the buddy on the workout.

We do not share your information with anyone outside the App except as described in Services We Use.

6. Keeping and Deleting Your Data

How long we keep it

  • Your account data is kept until you delete your account.
  • If you create an account but do not finish setting it up, we delete that account and its email address automatically after 30 days. The 30 days count from the day you created it or last signed in, whichever is later.
  • The copy of each notification kept inside the App is deleted after 7 days.
  • Invite-link IP records are deleted automatically 10 to 11 minutes after the request.
  • Crash reports are kept by Google, which governs how long.

Delete your account in the App

Open Gym Buddy, go to the Profile tab, tap Account, then Delete Account, type DELETE and tap Delete My Account. You are signed out and your account is removed straight away.

Delete your account without the App

Email danielsgymbuddy@gmail.com from the email address of the account, with the subject "Delete my Gym Buddy account". We delete the account within 30 days. See Delete your account.

What is deleted, and what stays

When your account is deleted, we permanently delete:

  • Your profile and sign-in account, including the Google name and picture address Supabase stored if you signed in with Google
  • Your workouts and check-ins, streaks and break days, experience points, coins and cosmetics
  • Your buddy team memberships, friendships, nicknames, nudges and invites you sent
  • Your device token and notification settings

Some information does not get deleted, because it belongs to shared records rather than to you alone:

  • A team's streak record stays with the remaining team members, no longer linked to you.
  • A workout your buddy created with you stays on your buddy's account, with the link to you removed.
  • Records held by Google, such as crash reports, are governed by Google.

7. Children

Gym Buddy is not for anyone under 16. The App asks for your age before you can finish creating an account and will not accept an age under 16, and our database rejects it too. The App cannot check that the age you enter is true. An account that is not finished is deleted after 30 days. If you believe a user is under 16, contact us and we will investigate and remove the account if confirmed.

8. Your Rights

You can ask us to:

  • Give you a copy of the information we hold about you
  • Correct information that is wrong
  • Delete your account and data
  • Restrict or object to our use of your information
  • Give it to you in a portable form

You can change your username in the App under Account and delete your account as described above. For anything else, email danielsgymbuddy@gmail.com. We answer rights requests within one month.

You also have the right to complain to your data protection authority. In Ireland this is the Data Protection Commission (dataprotection.ie).

9. International Transfers

Our data is stored with Supabase in London, United Kingdom. The Google services described above (push notifications, crash reports and sign-in) may process data outside the UK and the European Economic Area, under Google's own safeguards. See Google's Privacy Policy.

10. Security

Data travels between the App and our servers over encrypted connections (HTTPS), and the App refuses unencrypted traffic. Access to the database is limited by rules that keep each user to their own data and the data shared with them, and your password is handled by our sign-in provider, which means we cannot read it. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure.
