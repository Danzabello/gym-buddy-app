Privacy Policy for Gym Buddy

Last updated: August 12, 2026

Gym Buddy ("the App", "we", "us") is developed by Keep Going! ("the Developer"). This Privacy Policy explains what information the App collects, how it's used, who can see it, and what choices you have.

Gym Buddy is completely free to use. We do not sell your data, and we do not run ads.

If you have questions about this policy or want to exercise any of the rights described below, contact us at danielsgymbuddy@gmail.com.

1. Information We Collect

Account information

When you sign up, we collect an email address and password to create your account (handled securely by our authentication provider, Supabase — we never see or store your plain-text password).

Profile information

During onboarding, we collect:

  • Display name and username (required)
  • Age (required, minimum 16 — see Children's Privacy below)
  • Fitness goals and fitness level (required/self-selected)
  • Gender (optional)
  • Avatar and cosmetic preferences (a preset image you choose — we do not access your camera or photo library)
  • Timezone, detected automatically from your device, used to determine your daily streak and check-in windows correctly

Activity data

As you use the App, we store the fitness activity you log: workout type, category, date, time, planned and actual duration, check-in history, streak counts, and break days you take. If you write anything in an optional notes field, whatever you type there is stored as-is — please avoid including sensitive personal information (such as health conditions) in free-text fields, since we do not ask for or process that kind of data.

We do not collect weight, height, heart rate, calories, sleep, nutrition, or any medical or health-measurement data. Gym Buddy tracks activity scheduling and consistency, not health metrics.

Social data

Information related to your buddy connections: friend requests and friendships, buddy teams, invite codes you send or receive, and nudges sent between buddies.

Device and notification data

To send you push notifications (like a buddy checking in or a streak reminder), we store a device token issued by Firebase Cloud Messaging (see Third-Party Services below). This token is tied to your account and is removed when you sign out.

Automatically collected data

When you use an invite link, our system briefly logs the IP address making the request, solely to prevent abuse (automated guessing of invite codes). This data is automatically deleted within approximately 10 minutes and is never used for any other purpose.

What we don't collect

We do not access your device's location, camera, photo library, or contacts. We do not use any analytics, advertising, or crash-reporting services — Gym Buddy has no third-party tracking of any kind beyond what's described in this policy.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information above to:

  • Create and maintain your account
  • Operate the App's core features (streaks, check-ins, buddy pairing, teams)
  • Send push notifications you'd expect (buddy activity, streak status, friend requests)
  • Prevent abuse of features like invite links
  • Provide customer support if you contact us

We do not use your data for advertising, and we do not sell or rent your information to anyone.

3. Third-Party Services

Gym Buddy uses two infrastructure providers to operate:

Firebase Cloud Messaging (Google) delivers push notifications. To do this, Google receives your device's notification token, the notification text (which may include a buddy's display name and activity, such as "Alex wants to be your gym buddy!"), and a general event type. Google does not receive your email address, password, or internal account ID.

Supabase hosts our database, authentication, and backend logic. All of your account and activity data described in this policy is stored on Supabase's infrastructure, located in the United Kingdom (London region).

Because Gym Buddy is distributed through the Google Play Store, Google Play Services also operate on your device as part of standard app distribution and installation — this is governed by Google's own policies, separate from Gym Buddy.

We do not use any other third-party SDKs, analytics platforms, or advertising networks.

4. Coach Max

Coach Max is an automated in-app character that sends motivational tips and check-in reminders. Coach Max is not powered by artificial intelligence, and no data is sent to any AI service, external or otherwise — its messages are pre-written content selected and delivered automatically by our own systems. Notifications from Coach Max reach you through the same push notification system described above.

5. Who Can See Your Information

Gym Buddy is a social fitness app, and some information is visible to other users by design:

  • Your profile (display name, age, gender if provided, fitness goals, fitness level, and activity stats) is visible to any other signed-in user of the App, to support finding and connecting with workout buddies.
  • Check-in activity, streak status, and break days are visible to your confirmed buddies and teammates.
  • Team check-in history is visible to your teammates and your accepted (mutual) friends.
  • Live workout status (e.g., that you're mid-workout right now) is visible to a buddy you're actively paired with for that session.
  • Information you set privately — such as nicknames you give friends, or friend requests you've sent — is visible only to you.

We do not share your information with anyone outside the App except as described in Third-Party Services.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

You can delete your account at any time from within the App. When you do, we permanently delete:

  • Your profile and account credentials
  • Your activity data (workouts, check-ins, streaks, break days)
  • Your social data (friendships, nicknames, nudges, sent invites)
  • Your device token and notification settings

Some information does not get deleted, because it belongs to shared, ongoing records rather than to you individually:

  • Shared team streak history (e.g., your team's current or longest streak) is retained for the benefit of remaining team members, though it is no longer linked to your personal account.
  • If you were paired with a buddy on a shared workout, that workout record is retained on your former buddy's account, with your identifying information removed from it.

If you have questions about what happens to your data after deletion, contact us at danielsgymbuddy@gmail.com.

7. Children's Privacy

Gym Buddy is not intended for anyone under the age of 16. We require users to confirm they are 16 or older during sign-up, and our systems enforce this minimum. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 16. If you believe a user under 16 has created an account, please contact us and we will investigate and remove the account if confirmed.

8. Your Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights over your personal information, which can include the right to:

  • Access the information we hold about you
  • Correct inaccurate information
  • Delete your account and associated data (available directly in the App)
  • Object to or restrict certain processing
  • Data portability, where applicable

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at danielsgymbuddy@gmail.com. We will respond within a reasonable timeframe and in accordance with applicable law (including, where relevant, the UK/EU General Data Protection Regulation and other regional privacy laws).

9. Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your information, including encrypted data storage, access controls, and regular security review. However, no method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. If we make material changes, we will update the "Last updated" date above. Continued use of the App after changes take effect constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
